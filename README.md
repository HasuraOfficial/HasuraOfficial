## 🚀 What is Hasura?

**Hasura** is a multidisciplinary technology and creative studio built
around one simple idea:

> **Great ideas deserve to become real experiences.**

We work across technology and creativity to help businesses, startups,
students, creators and organizations bring their ideas to life.

From **apps and websites** to **software projects, UI/UX, events,
music, video and creative solutions**, we combine technical thinking
with creative execution.
